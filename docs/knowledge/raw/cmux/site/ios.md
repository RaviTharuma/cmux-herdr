# cmux iOS

[![cmux icon](https://cmux.com/logo.png)](https://cmux.com/)


Your terminals, in your pocket.

The cmux iOS app mirrors your Mac terminals to iPhone and iPad in realtime. Drive your coding agents, watch builds, and switch workspaces from anywhere, over your own Tailscale or local network.

## Features

-   **Realtime sync**: your terminals stream to iPhone and iPad the instant they change
-   **Bring your own network**: connect over your own Tailscale or local network, with no servers in between
-   **Vertical tabs**: every workspace one tap away, just like the desktop
-   **Agent notifications**: get pinged when Claude, Codex, or any agent needs your input
-   **Full keyboard**: hardware shortcuts and a terminal-grade input bar with arrows, tab, and modifiers
-   **Native and fast**: GPU-accelerated rendering on the same engine as the Mac app

## How it works

Pair your iPhone with your Mac from the Mobile Connect window, then attach to any workspace. The connection runs over your own Tailscale or local network, so your terminals never touch a third-party server.
